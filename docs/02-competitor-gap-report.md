# Competitor gap report

*Written 2026-09-07. Every competitor below was actually visited and read, and
every claim about them is something their own page says. Where a page could not
be read, that is stated rather than filled in.*

---

## Method, and its limits

The competitors that matter for SEO are the pages that **currently rank for the
queries Skilloura wants**, which is not the same thing as the businesses it
competes with for work. Two SERPs were examined:

1. `website development company Bhubaneswar Odisha` — the local commercial query
2. `restaurant website cost India` — representative of the cost-article cluster

**Limit worth stating:** these results came from a US-located search. Google
personalises by location, so the local pack and some organic ordering will
differ when searched from Odisha. Re-check from the actual location before
making a decision that depends on exact ranking order. The *content* findings
below are unaffected — those come from reading the pages themselves.

## 2. Where Skilloura is genuinely ahead

These are not aspirations. Each is already built and live in the repo.

**1. Published prices — nobody local does this.**

Neither Cakiweb nor AH publishes a figure. Both gate pricing behind an enquiry.
Skilloura publishes a starting price on every service, a full package table on
`/pricing`, and a price index that lets someone compare all nine services in one
view. For the searcher who is comparing four studios at eleven at night, three
of them require a phone call and one answers the question. That is the entire
argument, and it is already won.

**2. A written scope before payment.**

Cakiweb documents a *process*; nobody documents a *deliverable you receive
before paying*. Skilloura's `SampleDocs` shows the actual quotation, timeline,
revision checklist, handover checklist and invoice. This is the one thing on the
site that no competitor is showing, and it is the correct centre of the brand.

**3. Case studies with reasoning.**

Cakiweb's are "coming soon". AH has named projects with no narrative. Skilloura
now has six written up as decisions and the trade-offs accepted — and every one
links to a build the reader can open and use in the same tab.

**4. Explorable live demos.**

Sixty-three demo pages, nine working components. Nobody local has this. A
prospect can *use* the thing before enquiring.

**5. Honest performance numbers.**

Measured, with conditions attached. Competitors state none.

## 3. Where Skilloura is genuinely behind

Stating these plainly, because a gap report that only lists wins is marketing,
not analysis.

**1. Age and volume of real work.** AH has 13 years and 50+ named client
projects. Skilloura has concept builds. No amount of design or SEO closes this —
only shipped client work does. This is exactly why the case studies are framed
honestly rather than dressed up as client projects: a young studio pretending
otherwise is easy to check and destroys the one asset it has.

**2. No certifications.** Cakiweb's CMMI and ISO marks are a real advantage for
institutional buyers. Worth knowing that this segment is currently closed, and
deciding whether to pursue it or ignore it deliberately.

**3. No hosting or domain offering.** AH bundles it and gets recurring revenue
plus supplier lock-in. Skilloura sells maintenance plans but not hosting. Worth
considering — though it also means support obligations.

**4. No physical address published.** Both competitors show one; Skilloura shows
a service area. For a service-area business that is legitimate, but it does cost
something in local trust.

**5. No testimonials from real clients yet.** Neither competitor has written ones
either, so this is a tie rather than a loss — and it is a chance to get ahead,
since the review-collection flow at `/review/<token>` already works.

## 4. The cost-article SERP — and a positioning discovery

Page one for `restaurant website cost India` is **entirely agency blog posts**:
ColorWhistle, Ajroni, Webyking, GrowWithBA, RichMenu, Cybiqon. Not a single
service page.

Two things follow.

**The page type is settled.** For any `X cost in India` query, an article ranks
and a service page does not. Skilloura already has eleven such articles, and
this confirms they are the correct shape. Do not convert them to service pages.

**The prices those articles quote are for a different customer entirely.** One of
the ranking pages puts an Indian restaurant website at **₹2,20,000–₹4,20,000**.
Skilloura quotes ₹12,000–₹25,000 for the same category.

That is not a small difference and it is worth being deliberate about. Those
articles are written by agencies selling enterprise builds, and they are the
only answer a small restaurant owner currently finds. Someone with a ₹20,000
budget reads page one, concludes a website is out of reach, and stops looking.

**The opportunity:** an honest cost article that says a functioning restaurant
website starts around ₹12,000, explains exactly what changes the price, and is
frank about when you genuinely do need to spend more. It answers the question
the searcher actually has, and it is differentiated from every page currently
ranking — not by being cleverer, but by being about them.

`/blog/restaurant-website-cost-india` already exists. It should be checked
against this: does it lead with a real starting figure in the first hundred
words, and does it name the ₹2 lakh quotes as being for a different kind of
project? If not, that is the highest-value single edit available.
